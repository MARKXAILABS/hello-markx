<objective>
Undo two changes this phase made that each weakened the thing they were added to strengthen: a budget
arm that made the breaker's ceiling WEAKER, and a delivery queue loader that arms the write path after
a failed read.

Purpose: `01-REVIEW-a-main.md` `a/CR-04` and `a/CR-05`. `evaluate()` returns on the first matching arm,
and the FLOOR-10 budget arm sits above the per-agent cap, the floor `costCapUsd` cap, the floor token
cap, the velocity arm and the no-progress arm. Its lower band returns
`{ tripping: true, ceiling: 'steering' }`, which pins the ladder at rank 1 AND skips every arm below —
so an agent at 85% of its card cap that is also the floor's biggest spender over `costCapUsd` can no
longer be constrained. Before FLOOR-10 landed, that agent reached `constrained`.

`loadQueue` assigns `this.queueFile = path` on every path including after the `catch` swallowed the
failure — while the doc comment three lines above states the opposite invariant. One `EBUSY`/`EPERM`
(Windows AV or an indexer holding the file) or one `EMFILE` under load replaces the queue with `[]`,
and the next mutation writes the emptiness to disk.

Output: two small behavioural changes, each with a RED-first test, each with a negative control.

## What changed from revision 1, and why

Lenses 3 and 4 found no blocker here. Lenses 2 and 5 found five. All five are corrected below.

1. **Every frontmatter gate was re-measured and three were replaced.** `contains: BUDGET_STEER_FRACTION`
   returned **2 hits at HEAD** and `pattern: queueFile = null` returned **1 hit at HEAD** — both
   satisfied by `git checkout .`. `pattern: soft` returned 0 but was satisfiable by the doc-block
   rewrite this plan itself instructs. `contains: queueFile` on the test file pushed the test toward
   inspecting a private field, which task 2's own behaviour list forbids. The replacements are
   `softTrip`, `softTrip = `, `code !== 'ENOENT'`, `queueReadError` and `EISDIR` — **all five measured
   at 0 hits at HEAD a217018 this session.**
2. **The "byte-identical when the band is the only thing wrong" claim is withdrawn.** It is false. The
   no-progress arm is STATEFUL (`s.noProgressBeats += 1`), and with the band no longer returning early
   that counter accumulates on band-only beats. Task 1 now states the consequence, pins it with a test,
   and frames it correctly: FLOOR-10 introduced the mask, and removing the mask restores arms that
   already existed.
3. **The transient-failure refusal message is fixed in the same change.** Leaving `queueFile` null
   makes `enqueue` return *"no harness home — nowhere durable to park this"* — flatly wrong during an
   antivirus scan, and plan 01-28 renders main's `error` verbatim to the operator.
4. **A positive control was added to task 2.** Revision 1 asserted only that the bytes DO NOT change.
   A loader that never arms, ever, satisfied every criterion it stated.
5. **Three anchors were wrong and are corrected.** `index.ts:1635` is the breaker's INPUT
   (`budget: hive.budgetForAgent(id)`); the level is consumed at **`index.ts:1639`**. The ladder's
   ceiling is declared at **`breaker.ts:287`**, not `:283`. `delivery.ts:433-482` spans two methods —
   `removeQueued` starts at **`:477`**.
