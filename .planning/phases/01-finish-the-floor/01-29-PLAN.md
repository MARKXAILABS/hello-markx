above** — task 3's `<action>`, `<behavior>` and `<done>`, the `key_links` entry and the threat register

`actions/checkout@v4` steps in `.github/workflows/ci.yml` (`:22`, `:79`, `:121`) set no

gate on ubuntu, windows AND macOS — turning a RED-demo mechanism into a CI outage.

permanent second test case. `key_links[1].pattern` is `minWinWidthFromSource` accordingly, and the
