<objective>
Sweep the remaining 23 renderer files to the corrected type scale and give every icon-only button in
them an accessible name — completing the FLOOR-12 surface.

Purpose: FLOOR-12, group 4b of 5. Measured at plan time: **75 sub-14px `fontSize` sites across 23
files**, led by `UpdateToast.tsx` (6), `FileTree.tsx` (6), `AgentDetailPanel.tsx` (6) and
`AgentControlStrip.tsx` (6), with a long tail of 1-5 site files.

**Why this plan is one half of a pair.** The remaining-renderer surface is 31 files and ~160 sites.
Classifying all 31 and then editing 23 of them exceeds a single agent's context budget by roughly 2×,
and a sweep that runs out of context mid-file produces a *partial* sweep whose only failure surface is
plan 22's completeness bar in wave 9. So the surface is split on its natural boundary: plan 18 owns
the eight densest files, this plan owns the other 23. The two file sets are disjoint, both run in
wave 7, and neither depends on the other.

After plans 18 and 19 the renderer's components are final, which is why plan 20's lint gate and plan
21's static-render tests come next.

`01-UI-SPEC.md` § FLOOR-12 is the **binding** contract; its rules are mechanical and checked in order.

Output: 23 files clearing the floor, an accessible-name pass, and this sub-group's frozen Rule 0
allowlist handed to plan 22.
</objective>
