the wrong number twice over. The ledger holds **mixed row semantics** — `index.ts:1513` appends
cumulative per-beat snapshots while `hooks.ts:272` appends per-response deltas — and `taskSpend()`

early spend falls out of the window and `over` reads false when it is true. `db.ts:44` states the
intended contract verbatim. FLOOR-09 closes almost for free in the same edit, because
`telemetry.recordCostSample()` exists, is documented as exactly this wiring, and has zero production
callers.

breaker, and the test that encoded the defect rewritten — source first, in its own commit.
