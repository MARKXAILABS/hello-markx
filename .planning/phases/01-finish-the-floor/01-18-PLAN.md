<objective>
Sweep the eight highest-density remaining renderer files to the corrected type scale and give every
icon-only button in them an accessible name.

Purpose: FLOOR-12, group 4a of 5. Measured at plan time: **85 sub-14px `fontSize` sites across 8
files** — `SkillsTab.tsx` (16), `MessageQueueComposer.tsx` (16), `WorkersTab.tsx` (10),
`MemoryPanel.tsx` (9), `MemoryGraphPanel.tsx` (9), `App.tsx` (9), `CostHud.tsx` (8), `AskMeTab.tsx` (8).

**Why this plan is one half of a pair.** The remaining-renderer surface is 31 files and ~160 sites.
Classifying all 31 (rule table + hoisted-object consumer map + button inventory + lint anchors +
fixed-height integers) and then editing 23 of them exceeds a single agent's context budget by roughly
2×, and a sweep that runs out of context mid-file produces a *partial* sweep whose only failure
surface is plan 22's completeness bar in wave 9 — five waves and one full context reset later. So the
surface is split on its natural boundary: this plan owns the eight densest files, plan 19 owns the
other 23. The two file sets are disjoint, both run in wave 7, and neither depends on the other.

`01-UI-SPEC.md` § FLOOR-12 is the **binding** contract; its rules are mechanical and checked in order.

Output: 8 files clearing the floor, an accessible-name pass, and this sub-group's frozen Rule 0
allowlist handed to plan 22.
</objective>
