<objective>
Ship the client half of DAEMON-02: the five static files an Android phone installs, the VAPID sender
that wakes it, and the packaging entry without which none of it exists in the shipped app.

Purpose: DAEMON-02's operator-facing surface. The server half — path routing, the pairing exchange,
the ask list and the answer POST — is plan 05's, and is **already merged and curl-verified** by the
time this plan starts. This plan is written against that contract; it does not extend it, and it does
not touch `src/main/webhook.ts`.

**D-21 is binding on the shape.** This is not a route in the renderer. The renderer's main chunk was
measured at 12,228.98 kB and its entry is written against the preload `window.cth` bridge, which does
not exist in Chrome on Android — so "a route in the renderer" is not a cheaper phone surface, it is a
rewrite of the renderer's data layer. Five hand-written files, no framework, no bundler, no CDN.

**D-18 is binding on the claim.** Android Chrome will not install a PWA from a non-secure origin and
`http://192.168.x.x` is not one. The phone is reachable only over the tunnel plan 04 opened. There is
no LAN-only fallback in this plan, and a browser-branded home-screen shortcut is not a PWA.

Output: `resources/phone/{index.html,sw.js,manifest.webmanifest,icon-192.png,icon-512.png}`, a
`src/main/push.ts` that is `node:crypto` and nothing else, the `extraResources` entry plus the
`build-assets` clauses that keep it honest, and a SUMMARY whose limitation ledger is longer than its
completion claim — because on this requirement, without a device, that is the true shape.
</objective>
