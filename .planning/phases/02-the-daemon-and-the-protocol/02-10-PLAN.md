<objective>
Make the public tunnel impossible to miss and its phone pairing impossible to fumble: a titlebar chip
that exists only while the tunnel is up, and a Settings → Connections panel carrying the untruncated
URL, the ephemerality notice, and a permanent QR that re-mints itself the instant the host changes.

Purpose: **DAEMON-05, closed.** This plan sits at wave 8, after the phone (plan 09), and not beside
it, for one reason: the requirement's text is *"the same PWA is reachable **over the public tunnel**"*.
A UI plan that ticked DAEMON-05 in a wave where the PWA had not landed would be closing a requirement
it cannot demonstrate. So this plan's **first verification step is the live loop** — tunnel up → QR →
plan 09's phone shell answering over the public origin — and every other criterion here is
subordinate to it.

**"Always visible" is a security property expressed as a UI one.** That is why it is a persistent
titlebar chip rendered only while the tunnel is up — absence is the signal — and never a toast, which
is dismissible, auto-expires, and is gone on the next launch: a toast satisfies the words and not the
property.

Output: a vendored MIT QR encoder with a digest that goes red on drift, one SVG component, one panel,
one chip with two measured degradation steps, and a SUMMARY that states plainly which half of
DAEMON-05's wording is met and which is not (D-41).
</objective>
