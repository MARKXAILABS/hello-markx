<objective>
Make the floor survive having no window — and close the two mail gaps that make "runs headless" a
claim rather than a behaviour.

Purpose: DAEMON-01, plus the STRUCT-01 row (`test/agent-lifecycle.test.cjs`) that neither frozen plan
claimed. **D-09 changes what this plan is.** A `--headless` flag on its own produces a floor that
cannot be quit: `index.ts:5783-5790` `preventDefault`s on live PTYs and then asks a renderer that does
not exist. And **D-11 changes what the requirement's content is.** The webContents/setInterval census
is not the audit — two IPC-*push* listeners it cannot see are the ones that break the floor: every
piece of mail to a hookless or proxy-tier engine bounces to the god (`hive.ts:1596-1620`), and a Crush
agent spawns having never learned the hive protocol. Criterion 2 says "mail still routes between
them". Both of those fail it.

So this plan is: the decision extracted to a pure predicate module, four small Electron edits that
call it, both mail gaps routed through `delivery.enqueue()` — main's one PTY-write gate — the last two
renderer typers deleted, ADR-0001 amended to name where that gate actually lives, and the agent
lifecycle finally driven by a test.

Output: one new predicate module, one new lifecycle module, a floor that quits, a floor whose mail
moves with the window shut, and three limitations written down instead of ticked.
</objective>
