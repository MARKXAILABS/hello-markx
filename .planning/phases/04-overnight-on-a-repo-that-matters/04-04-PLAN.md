<objective>
VIGIL-04's clock, and the one interface field VIGIL-02 needs. Both live in the same forty lines of
`src/main/hive.ts` (`:110-152`), which is why they are one plan: two plans editing the same interface
declaration under `use_worktrees: false` is a merge conflict by construction (D-35, and the Collision Map
scores `hive.ts` as a three-way file).

**Purpose.** `HiveTask` (`hive.ts:119-152`) has `createdAt: string` and **no `updatedAt`** — confirmed
exactly as REQUIREMENTS.md states. Without it, "a card nine hours in `doing`" is unmeasurable and
VIGIL-04's whole criterion has no data. The convention to match is already present three times over:
`HumanQA` carries `askedAt` / `answeredAt` / `dismissedAt` (`hive.ts:111-117`) and `HiveTask.review`
carries `askedAt` (`:149`), all optional ISO strings (D-30).

**The subtlety that a TASK_CLI-only implementation gets wrong.** PROTOCOL.md and `hive.ts:2810` name
`bin/task.cjs` the ledger's **only sanctioned writer**, and D-30 concludes "which makes it the one place
to stamp." `04-RESEARCH.md` § Pattern 10 measured that this is not the whole truth: `hive.ts:2141-2180`
also mutates the ledger from main — the kanban UI, inbound webhooks and Slack all write through
`HiveManager` (the god's own prompt says so at `hive.ts:1471`). **Both writers must stamp**, or the age
is measured from the wrong clock in exactly the case where a human touched the card.

**This plan writes no release logic and renders nothing.** `HiveTask.released` is declared here and
written by plan 04-08 (`floor/lifecycle.ts`); the age and the `DROPPED BY` row are rendered by plan 04-12.
Declaring the shape here is the interface-first ordering that keeps three plans from exploring the
codebase for a contract.

**Output.** Two optional fields on `HiveTask`, stamping in both writers, tests over all four verbs.
