`MAX_BODY_BYTES`. GATE-05's *"the operator is asked wherever they are, including on the phone"* is
**publishing into that list**. Build no second one.

**D-10 — approvals are a registry merged into `openAsks()`, and they do NOT become cards.**
`openPhoneAsks()` (`index.ts:1219-1236`) walks the task ledger and yields one ask per blocked card with an
open `humanQA` entry — it is card-derived and keyed on `taskId`. A tool approval has no card, is
ephemeral, and expires to deny in seconds. Writing one into `tasks.json` would block a real card, survive
its own timeout as a dead question, and put a high-frequency transient into the durable ledger ADR-0004
has a single committer for.

**L-12 / rule G-1 — the wire field stays `taskId`, and this is locked.** `resources/phone/index.html`
contains `taskId` **21 times** and `resources/phone/sw.js` **10 times**. `sw.js` is **installed on the
operator's phone** and updates on its own schedule; a mismatched service worker is a live-device failure
with no local reproduction. Take D-10's own escape hatch: add `kind: 'card' | 'tool'` and keep the wire
field named `taskId`, carrying the ask id for tool approvals. **Zero renames.**

**Output.** Two optional `PhoneAsk` fields, a widened `answerAsk` return, the registry merge, the phone's
two new renderings, one line of `sw.js`, and the CSP hash re-run.
