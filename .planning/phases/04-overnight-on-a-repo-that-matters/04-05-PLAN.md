<objective>
GATE-02: bound the blast radius of every agent shell at the one place a PTY's environment is built.

**Purpose.** `src/main/pty.ts:745` is the single `pty.spawn` call, and `:751` opens its env with
`...process.env` before layering `PATH`, `TERM`, `COLORTERM`, `FORCE_COLOR`, the POSIX locale pair, the
per-agent `opts.env`, and finally `HIVE_SOCK_TOKEN` and the OTLP header tokens — the last two deliberately
last "so nothing upstream can shadow it". One choke point, already commented as such, already ordered. The
fix is a **filter applied to the base spread**, not a new mechanism (D-11, RESEARCH § Pattern 1).

**Allowlist, not denylist, and the reasoning is already in the codebase.** The operator's credentials
arrive under names this project does not choose — `AWS_*`, `GH_TOKEN`, `GOOGLE_*`, `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`,
`OPENAI_API_KEY`, `NPM_TOKEN`, anything ending `_SECRET`, and whatever the next tool invents. Enumerating
an input space someone else chooses is the exact reasoning `hooks.ts:815` already rejects for path
spellings.

**S-7 is why this plan is wave 1.** *"GATE-02 is first or last within the GATE track, never in the
middle — stripping the env is the most likely way to break a working floor, and it must be the change a
live smoke test immediately follows. Mid-wave, every subsequent failure is ambiguous."* This plan is
first, and it carries its own live smoke as its last task. That is why `autonomous: false`: task 3 is a
blocking human verification, and it is adjacent to the change by design, not by accident.

**Output.** `allowFromEnv` in `shellEnv.ts` with a written ceiling, the splice at `pty.ts:751`, an
operator-visible pass-through escape, a both-directions unit test, an extended PTY test, and a live smoke.
</objective>
